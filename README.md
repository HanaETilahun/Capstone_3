## Capstone 3 – EmporiUm Sales Analysis (West Region)

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Year Up United Data Analytics Training Academy Week 12 Capstone. The objective was to analyze four years of sales data for EmporiUm, a virtual student bookstore that combines in-store and online sales. The analysis focuses on the West Region and provides insights that can help regional directors, territory managers, and store managers make informed business decisions.

Using Power BI, I cleaned, transformed, modeled, and visualized sales data from multiple sources to create an interactive report highlighting sales trends, category performance, regional sales distribution, and top-selling books.

## Business Scenario

EmporiUm was formed in 2022 through the merger of a college bookstore chain and an online retailer specializing in books and technology products. As the company continues to grow, leadership requested an analysis of sales performance over the last four years to identify key trends and opportunities within each sales region.

As the assigned data analyst for the West Region, I was responsible for creating a report that would help the regional sales team better understand sales performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were:

* Analyze sales trends across the entire reporting period
* Compare sales performance across product categories
* Evaluate sales distribution within the West Region
* Identify top-selling general audience books and their authors
* Provide actionable insights for regional management

## Data Preparation

Several data-cleaning and transformation steps were performed in Power Query, including:

* Importing and reviewing sales data files
* Removing unnecessary columns
* Correcting data types
* Creating relationships between tables
* Merging product and sales information
* Creating date fields for trend analysis
* Filtering and categorizing book sales
* Preparing author information for top-selling book analysis

## Data Model

The report uses a relational data model consisting of sales, product, location, and book information tables. Relationships were established to support accurate reporting and efficient analysis.

## Report Pages

### Page 1: West Region Sales Performance Overview

This page provides a high-level view of regional sales performance and includes:

* Line chart showing sales trends over time
* Bar chart displaying sales by product category
* Donut chart showing relative sales by state
* Key performance indicators and summary insights

### Page 2: Top-Selling Books Analysis

This page focuses on general audience bookstore products and includes:

* Top-selling books in the West Region
* Author names associated with each title
* Sales rankings and performance comparisons
* Insights into customer purchasing preferences

## Key Insights

The report highlights important sales trends and product performance patterns within the West Region. These insights can help management:

* Identify strong and weak sales periods
* Understand which categories generate the most revenue
* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products
* Improve inventory planning
* Support future sales growth strategies
